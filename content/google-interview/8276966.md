# Google L4 Interview Questions (Graphs, System Design)

## Questions

### Data Structures & Algorithms · Medium
Given a dataset of movies with ratings and a similarity graph between them, design an algorithm to find and return the top K most similar movies for a given movie, ranked by their rating.

**Approach:** Perform a graph traversal (like BFS or DFS) starting from the given movie to find all similar movies. Use a min-heap of size K to keep track of the top K movies encountered so far based on their rating.

### System Design & Coding · Hard
Design an `AdServer` data structure that supports `insertAd(content, score)` and `getAd()`. The `getAd()` method must return the ad with the highest score, then decrease its score, and ensure the same ad is not returned on two consecutive calls. A follow-up added a cooldown period.

**Approach:** Use a max-heap (Priority Queue) to efficiently retrieve the highest-scored ad in O(log N). To handle the "no consecutive" rule, when an ad is served, temporarily hold it back and insert the next-best ad from the heap. Then, re-insert the held-back ad into the heap for future consideration. A hash map is needed for score updates.

### Data Structures & Algorithms · Medium
Given a 2D grid of land and water, design an algorithm to find all 'lakes' (bodies of water completely surrounded by land and not touching the grid's border).

**Approach:** Iterate through the grid. When a water cell is found, start a traversal (BFS/DFS). During the traversal, if you hit the border, it's not a lake. If the traversal completes without hitting a border, all visited water cells form a lake. To avoid re-processing, mark visited cells.
