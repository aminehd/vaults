[Scale AI](https://leetcode.com/discuss/topic/scale-ai/)

Just wanted to share my recent interview experience with Scale AI, hoping it might help others and maybe get some feedback.

**Background:**

A recruiter reached out to me on LinkedIn for a backend role at Scale AI. The company seemed promising, with a strong valuation, so I decided to give it a shot.

* * *

### 1\. Phone Screening

**Party Games:**

- **a.** Find all time zones for a zone → mapped to city → where party is going on.
- **b.** Find all dead zone hours for a zone (dead zone = where party is not going on, between first started party to latest ended party).

* * *

### 2\. Backend Practical Question

This round was pretty much as described in the prep materials.

- **Step 1:** Use Google Places API to convert resort names and Joey’s home address into place IDs.
- **Step 2:** Use the Routes API to determine the driving time between each pair of locations.
- **Step 3:** Given the place IDs and travel times, find the optimal route to minimize travel time (classic Travelling Salesman Problem).

I completed parts 1 and 2, and for part 3, I explained my approach to solving TSP. I was pretty confident since I managed to get through these parts quickly—honestly, I felt only a bot could finish all three in the given time.

* * *

**Result:**

Despite this, I got a rejection email from the recruiter. I’ve reached out to HR for feedback on what went wrong.

If they didn’t select me even after completing those parts in a short span, maybe they were looking for a bot-level performance or something else entirely.

* * *

**Takeaway:**

Not sure what more I could have done, but sharing here in case anyone has insights or similar experiences. Would love to hear your thoughts!

* * *

_TL;DR: Completed technical round at Scale AI quickly, still got rejected. Wondering if anyone else has faced something similar or has advice._
